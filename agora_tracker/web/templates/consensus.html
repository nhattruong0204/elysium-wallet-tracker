Consensus

Tokens that several tracked wallets net-bought within one window. Each card opens when the threshold is reached and lists every wallet that joined while it stayed active. Net buy = verified buys minus sells, so a wallet that dumps drops out.

Statistics, not advice. Several addresses can belong to one operator: same-block buyers and known bundle wallets are counted once in independent. So far most “3 independent” signals in the forward paper log fell below their entry: measure before you trust.
