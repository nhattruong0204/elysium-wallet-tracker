Wallet clusters

Groups of public wallets that left observable on-chain evidence of behaving as one operator would: buying the same token in the same block, moving a token to each other inside one transaction, or entering the same selective tokens within seconds. A cluster is a research aid for counting independent actors honestly. It says nothing about who controls the wallets and is not an allegation: it can be a team, a shared bot or copy-trading.
