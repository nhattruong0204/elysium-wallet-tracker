Method and limits

Who is “tracked”

The tracker follows wallets on Agora's public top-PnL leaderboard on Elysium (chain {{ cfg.chain.chain_id }}), refreshed every few minutes. Everything on this site describes those wallets only, not the whole chain. A wallet is qualified when the latest leaderboard snapshot before the window ends shows positive Agora-reported PnL. Wallets followed privately by the operator are never shown, counted or used in any number here: the database role behind this site cannot read them.

Verified, estimated, unknown

Verified HYPE amounts were measured on-chain from the transaction itself. Estimated amounts come from a model when the HYPE leg could not be measured. They are listed next to the verified numbers and never added to them. Transfers between wallets are not purchases. Where nothing could be determined the value is shown as unknown.

Two kinds of PnL

Agora PnL is the figure Agora reports for a wallet since it began trading. Measured PnL is realized profit the tracker computed from sells with a known cost basis inside the selected window. They answer different questions and are never combined.

Market data

Prices, market caps and changes are Agora's numbers captured by a collector about every ten minutes and stored. This site never queries Agora or the chain when you load a page, so figures carry the snapshot time and can be minutes old. Market caps are shown in USD: Agora quotes them in HYPE, and each snapshot is converted with the HYPE/USD rate Agora reported closest to that snapshot’s time (stored every ~5 minutes), so a past market cap does not change when HYPE moves. If no rate was stored within an hour of a snapshot, the market cap is shown as unavailable rather than estimated. Trade sizes and net flows stay in HYPE.

Time

All times are UTC. A window is half-open, [start, end): it includes the start instant and excludes the end instant. Rolling presets end at the page-load time rounded down to 30 seconds. Results are cached for 30 seconds.

Limits

  • Coverage starts when the tracker started; earlier activity is not backfilled. Windows reaching back past that say so.
  • Observed activity lags the wallets it describes. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell.
  • Small markets can be wash-traded or dominated by one wallet. Flags call this out when the data shows it.

API

JSON versions of each page live under /api/v1/ with the same parameters (range, start, end, page, sort). Decimals are strings to keep exact values.
